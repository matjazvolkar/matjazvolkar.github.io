Rules that apply to every option

  1. The spinner waits for the real server reply. The confirmation appears only after the request is saved. On failure, show “We couldn't send this. Call us on +386 …” instead of a thank-you.
  2. A tap pauses the tour. Closing the sheet without sending offers the existing “Take control / Continue tour” choice.
  3. On desktop, put the ribbon outside the phone frame. On the right edge of the bezel it costs no screen width and never covers app content. Inside the screen only on a real phone.
  4. Keep the label to 18 characters or fewer. Vertical text is read slowly; the icon (phone for Tier 1, spark for Tier 2) tells half the story before the words do.
  5. Prefill what you already know. Tier 1 has the phone number from the dossier, Tier 2 can prefill the email from the outreach link. Every empty field costs submissions.
  6. Gold means “talk to Nevron”. Tour buttons stay steel grey. Every sales action, ribbon included, is gold, so the eye learns one color for one meaning.
  7. Never call the app itself free. In Tier 2 the build of the demo is free. “Free app” creates a price shock on the first call.
